# NL Zepto — User Research Summary

**Method:** first-party survey (14 Zepto users) + 4,398 public Zepto reviews (Play Store, App Store, YouTube), July 2026.

---

## How people order

- Order 2–4× a week, mostly evenings
- "Milk, bread, snacks" — the basket barely changes
- **57% mainly use Zepto for groceries** — most journeys never leave grocery
- Reordering is a reflex, not a decision

## Pain points

- Trust breaks on fresh items:
  - *"Every pack of milk — one pack was spoiled, and fruits almost in bad condition"*
  - *"All my issues with stale veggies delivered have never been resolved"*
- One bad non-grocery order ends exploration:
  - *"They send rotten carrot but denied refund saying they delivered fresh carrots"*
- Nothing prompts a new category:
  - *"Best offers — sometimes products are out of stock"*
  - Offers push products, not plans

## Good things

- Speed is the habit-maker: *"Fast. Good quality products. Good service."*
- Zepto is the default for "I need it now"
- App is easy; checkout is fast

## The gap

- **13 of 14 know** Zepto sells other categories → awareness is not the problem
- **71% would explore more if it matched their goal** → interest exists, discovery doesn't
- Users shop in *missions* (a trip, a party, a recipe) — the app only sees items

## What this points to

- Meet the user at their **goal**, not the shelf
- Read the basket → name the mission → complete it across categories
- → This is what **Smart Missions** does
