# 10-Minute Zepto User Interview Kit

Run this with 6 real Zepto users (colleagues, flatmates, family). ~10 min each.
Record or take rough notes — short phrases are enough; I'll format them after.

## Questions

1. How often do you order on Zepto? What time of day, usually?
2. What did you order the last 3 times?
3. When did you last buy something that *wasn't* groceries on Zepto? What made you?
4. Walk me through your last annoying Zepto experience.
5. What's the one thing that keeps you coming back?
6. You're hosting friends this weekend — where do you shop for it, and why?
7. Did you know Zepto sells [electronics / party supplies / pet care]? Would you buy them there?
8. If the app knew what you were planning (a trip, a party), would you want it to help? How?
