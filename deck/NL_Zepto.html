How it happens

Open, grab the few things top of mind, checkout. The trip stays inside one or two categories and never wanders.

The result

Most trips touch only one or two categories. Travel, health, home and personal care sit one tap away and stay unopened.

The impact for Zepto

Fewer customers ever try a new category. Baskets stay small and the monthly growth goal stalls.

06 Why AI belongs here

Zepto sees what you searched, but never the goal you came in for

Grocery loyalists are not blocked by choice. They are blocked by an app that reads products, not missions, so the goals that would span categories stay invisible.
Target user
⭐ Grocery loyalists
Shop multiple times every week.
Arrive with one clear mission.
Already trust Zepto.
Rarely browse.
Keep the shopping list in their head.
They don't need product discovery. They need mission completion.
Root cause chain
1
The shopping goal exists first
"A weekend trip. Friends over. A house move."
▼
2
The app only sees products
Chips, cake, sunscreen, cleaning spray. Never the occasion.
▼
3
The goal is never understood
Nothing links the cart to the mission behind it.
▼
4
Discovery never happens
Travel, Party, Home and Electronics items stay invisible.
▼
5
The shopper finishes elsewhere
The rest of the mission gets bought on another app.
The result

Every visit reinforces grocery-only behaviour, because the broader mission is never recognised.

Root cause summary
The app understands products.
The shopper thinks in missions.
Nothing connects the two.
The missing input isn't a recommendation engine. It's understanding intent before checkout.
Product implication
Understand the mission.
Predict the goal behind the cart.
Ask one lightweight confirmation.
Then complete the mission.
AI appears only when confidence is high, and never assumes. The shopper stays in control.
The job of AI is not to recommend more products. It is to understand the shopping goal first, then quietly complete it.
